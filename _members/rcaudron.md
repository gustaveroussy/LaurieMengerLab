Romane is a research engineer in Laurie Menger’s team. She trained as a biotechnology engineer at SupBiotech and first joined the team for a six-month internship. Throughout her academic training, she has developed a strong interest in research, particularly in the field of immuno-oncology.

Her previous internships at the University of Rouen and the Henri Becquerel Cancer Center strengthened her laboratory skills and her interest in translational research. Through her work in the Menger team, she aims to further develop her skills in advanced cellular therapies research and contribute to projects at the interface of immunology and cancer.
